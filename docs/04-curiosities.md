# What the disc reveals

* **`project_ray`**. `Event/voice/adx_copy.bat` and `adx_copy2.bat`, a
  developer's copy scripts shipped on the disc, copy voice files from
  `Q:\Data\voice\event` and `Q:\Data\voice\face_chat\…` into
  `C:\project_ray\Program\dvddata\Event\voice\`: the game's working title,
  and its data tree as the disc keeps it (`dvddata` is the disc's root).
  `adx_copy2.bat` copies three single lines of "face chat" (FC00, FC03,
  FC06): a late fix of three voice lines.
* `_sasaki/` holds two small fonts (`font5x12.wtm`, `font5x9.wtm`), a
  developer's; `_viewer/` a viewer's data (`viewer.vol`, `enc_eff00.vol`).
* The Home Button menu's archives in six languages (English, French,
  German, Italian, Dutch, Spanish) for a North American disc.
